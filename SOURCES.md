## Our own design choices (not taken from the literature)
- Readiness thresholds: CAUTION if reaction time > +10% vs. personal baseline or 2–3 lapses; NOT READY if > +20% or ≥ 4 lapses. Lapse cutoffs are task-specific in the literature, so these are prototype values to be calibrated.
- 3-day escalation rule to flight surgeons.
- Heart-rate band 55–100 bpm (lower bound relaxed for fit astronauts).
- Guidance texts: 20–26 min nap and retest after 4 hours.
- "+4% reaction time per missing hour of sleep below 6 h" in the simulated data.
- 30-second demo mode: for demonstration only; lapse cutoffs are calibrated for the 3-minute test.
